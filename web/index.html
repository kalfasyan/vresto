    Paste a full Sentinel-2 L2A name, or just its start up to the tile: the date and tile are enough.

    Vresto Lite is a slice of Vresto. The full app is free and open source, and runs on your computer:

    • 13 map layers: land cover, elevation, NDVI, temperature, soil moisture, water bodies and burned area, with a value inspector
    • Pixel history: chart NDVI at any point over months or years, then export it as CSV
    • Downloads: any band at any resolution, plus quicklooks and metadata, with a free Copernicus account
    • Coverage grid: MGRS tiles coloured by how many products cover your dates
    • More data and tools: Sentinel-2 Level-1C and other Copernicus collections, a full-resolution tile server, a Python API and vresto-cli

    Install and start it:

    pip install vresto
